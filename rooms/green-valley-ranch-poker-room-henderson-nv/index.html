Notes
Green Valley Ranch is a Station Casinos locals casino in Henderson, about 15 minutes southeast of the Las Vegas Strip. The poker room reopened on December 18, 2025 as part of a $200 million property-wide renovation, returning to its original location near the sportsbook. The newly renovated room has 15 tables with leather rail tops, comfortable seating with built-in charging ports, and upgraded audio-visual systems. It is Henderson's only 24/7 poker room. Games include no-limit hold'em, limit hold'em, pot-limit Omaha, and Omaha hi/lo. The room participates in Station Casinos' shared promotions including the Jumbo Hold'em Bad Beat Jackpot.
