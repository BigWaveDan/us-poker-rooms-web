Notes
Caesars Republic Lake Tahoe (formerly Harveys Lake Tahoe) is a major casino resort on the Nevada side of South Lake Tahoe in Stateline. The property was rebranded from Harveys to Caesars Republic on July 1, 2025 as part of a $200 million renovation. The casino features a World Series of Poker Room with 16 tables, spreading no-limit and limit Texas Hold'em. The WSOP Circuit makes an annual stop here. The poker room opens daily in the afternoon and closes at 2:00 AM. The property is undergoing Phase Two renovations through mid-2026 but remains fully operational.
