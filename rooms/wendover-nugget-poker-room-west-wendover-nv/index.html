Notes
The Wendover Nugget Hotel and Casino is located in West Wendover, Nevada, right on the Utah-Nevada border about 120 miles west of Salt Lake City. The casino operates Ace's Poker Room with 8-9 tables, making it the largest poker room in the Wendover area. The room spreads no-limit and limit Texas Hold'em cash games and has offered tournaments in the past. It features a dedicated beverage station for poker players and is located adjacent to the Golden Harvest Cafe. The room draws players primarily from the Salt Lake City metro area. The property is operated by Maverick Gaming.
