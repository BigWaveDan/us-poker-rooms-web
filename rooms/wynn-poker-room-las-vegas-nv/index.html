Notes
The Wynn poker room is located at Encore, occupying 8,600 square feet adjacent to the Encore Casino floor. It features 28 tables equipped with USB charging ports and automatic shufflers, along with 37 65-inch high-definition televisions streaming live sports. The room runs no-limit hold'em from $1/$3 up to $10/$25, pot-limit Omaha at $1/$2 and $5/$5, and $9/$18 mixed games. A separate tournament area can expand to as many as 200 tables for major series. The room does not take a jackpot drop, so there are no high-hand or bad-beat jackpot promotions. The rake is 10% up to $5.
